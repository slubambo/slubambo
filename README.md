## What I Do

I am a senior full-stack developer and software team lead with deep experience building enterprise systems for higher education. My strongest work is at the intersection of software engineering, institutional operations, and applied data science.

At Uganda Christian University, I lead and contribute hands-on to a custom Management Information System built from scratch. The system supports admissions, student records, registration, results, transcripts, finance, HR, payroll, budgeting, timetabling, reporting, evaluations, permissions, audit, and integrations.

I recently completed an MSc in Data Science and Analytics. My thesis, **"Predicting Final CGPA Using Pre-Admission Data,"** applied machine learning, SHAP explainability, fairness checks, sensitivity analysis, and API/dashboard integration planning to historical UCU student data for proactive academic advising.
